Chronicle

One living record of an event, from the first idea to the last lesson.

Today an exercise lives in pieces: a scenario document, a network build, injects in a spreadsheet, activity in logs, lessons in someone’s notes. Chronicle is one record for the whole event, whether training, exercise or simulation. It begins as the scenario: what the world looks like, and what the event must achieve. Around it, apps publish and subscribe: each one subscribes to the parts it needs and publishes what it built, did and saw. When the event ends, the Chronicle is the event: complete, attributable, and ready to seed the next one.

Four Principles

01

One Record, Many Authors

Each app owns its part. It reads the rest, but writes only what it did.

02

Publish and Subscribe

Apps subscribe to the parts they need and publish what they add. None of them call each other.

03

Nothing Is Overwritten

Every change keeps who made it, when and why, so the record is also the event’s history.

04

The Right Eyes Only

Ground truth stays with exercise control. Participants see only what they should, everywhere.

Why It Matters for AI

AI is only as good as its context. A Chronicle is all the context.

A language model doesn’t know your exercise. Asked cold, it invents: a news story that contradicts the ground truth, a simulated bank clerk who doesn’t know which bank they work for, an after-action summary that can’t tell what was planned from what happened. Give it the Chronicle and it stops guessing. The record holds the world, the intent, what was built and everything that happened, each part marked with who wrote it and who may see it.

Because the record is structured, each AI is handed exactly the parts its job needs, not a pile of documents. That matters most on a closed range, where a smaller local model has room for less. And because visibility travels with the data, an AI playing a member of the public never learns the twist the participants are meant to find. Every event makes the next one smarter: the lessons of the last Chronicle are context for the next scenario.
